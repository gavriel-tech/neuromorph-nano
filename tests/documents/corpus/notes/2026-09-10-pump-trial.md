# Pump trial, day 1

Bench test pressure set to 4.2 bar for all six pumps.

All six primed on the first try except pump 3, which needed two attempts (air leak at the suction
union, retaped and fine).

Flow at 4.2 bar, litres per minute:
- pump 1: 39
- pump 2: 40
- pump 3: 38
- pump 4: 39
- pump 5: 41
- pump 6: 37

Pump 6 is slower because of the vessel on the outlet; expected.
