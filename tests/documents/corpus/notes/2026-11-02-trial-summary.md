# Trial summary

## Scope
Six AQ-40 pumps, eight weeks, two benches and two sites.

## Results
- Median flow at 3.8 bar: 41 litres per minute across all pumps.
- One seal failure (pump 4, week 2), cause a chipped ceramic seat.
- One capacitor failure (pump 1, week 8).
- One false fault stop (pump 2, code 5), cause a loose sensor cable.
- Scale reduced the flow of pump 3 at site B by about 10 percent in five weeks.

## What we change
- Descale every 60 days in hard water.
- Clean strainers weekly in the first two months on a new site.
- Keep at least 12 seal kits in stock.
