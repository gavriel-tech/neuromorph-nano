# Kickoff: AQ-40 field trial

Six AQ-40 pumps go on the test bench and then to two sites for an eight week trial.
Goal: find out how often the pumps need service in hard water, and which spares we must stock.

Roles:
- workshop lead: plans the trial and signs off decisions
- bench tech: runs the bench and keeps the log
- stores: counts parts and places orders

Pumps are numbered 1 to 6. Pumps 1 to 4 are base AQ-40, pump 5 is an AQ-40S, pump 6 is an AQ-40T.
