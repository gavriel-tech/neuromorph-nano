# Reading group: simple statistics

We read chapter 2 of the textbook (simple statistics) to plan how to report the trial.

Points we agreed:
- report the median flow per pump, not the mean, because one bad reading pulls the mean
- give the IQR next to the median
- flag outliers with the 1.5 IQR rule and check each one before setting it aside

Someone asked whether 12 readings per pump is enough. Agreed it is enough for a median, not for a
standard deviation we would trust. Revisit after the trial.
