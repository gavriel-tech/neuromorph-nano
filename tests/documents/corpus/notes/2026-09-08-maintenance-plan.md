# Maintenance plan for the trial pumps

Decisions for the eight week trial:

- Clean the inlet strainer every week (the manual says monthly, but the yard water is dirty).
- Descale every 90 days. The trial is shorter than that, so no descaling is planned unless flow drops.
- Check seals for drips at every strainer clean.
- Log every service action with the pump number.

Owner: workshop lead.
