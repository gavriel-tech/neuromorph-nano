# Bench test pressure lowered

From today the bench test pressure is 3.8 bar, not 4.2 bar.

Reason: both trial sites run at about 3.5 to 4 bar, and pump 6 kept tripping code 3 at the higher
setting because its vessel was charged for a lower cut-in. Testing at 3.8 bar matches the sites.

All flow readings from now on are at 3.8 bar. Earlier readings stay in the log but are not compared
directly with the new ones.
