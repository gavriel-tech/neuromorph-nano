# Seal kit supplier switch

S014 lead time has gone from 10 to 25 working days. That is too long for a part we change at short
notice.

Decision: from today seal kits are ordered from supplier S022. Their lead time is 6 working days and
the price is 19.10 per kit. S014 stays on the list as a backup.

First order with S022: 15 kits.
