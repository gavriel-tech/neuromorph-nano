# Pump 4 seal failure

The pump 4 drip turned into a steady leak overnight. Seal replaced today with a kit from the
first S014 order (see [[2026-09-02-supplier-call]]).

Findings when the old seal came out:
- the ceramic seat had a chip on its edge
- the carbon ring was fine

Likely cause: the seat was pushed in crooked at the factory, or it was knocked in transport.
After the new seal, ran for ten minutes, no leak. Took 50 minutes in all.
