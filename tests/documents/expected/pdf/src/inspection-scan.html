Pressure vessel inspection record

This record is kept with the AQ-40 service file. The inspection of the pressure vessel is carried out once a year by the installer, who checks the air charge, the membrane and the mounting bracket.

The next page is a scanned copy of the paper form signed at the last visit. It is a picture only and holds no text that a reader can take from it.

Notes after the inspection

The air charge was found at 1.3 bar and was raised to the correct value. The membrane showed no sign of wear. The mounting bracket bolts were tightened to 10 N m.
