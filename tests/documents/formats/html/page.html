AQ-40 Quick Care Guide

This page sums up the routine care of the AQ-40 pump. The full procedures are in the service manual.

Cleaning the strainer

Clean the inlet strainer every three months, or sooner on dirty water & after a flood.

  1. Switch the pump off at the isolator.
  2. Close the inlet valve and unscrew the strainer cap.
  3. Rinse the mesh under running water.
Never run the pump with the strainer removed.
